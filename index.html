Learn • Practical Guides

Practical Guides & Safety Tips

Practitioner-written technology, electrical safety and solar guides for homes, schools and businesses — part of the LEARN pillar.

💡 Quick Tech Tips

🔐
Always Verify Before You Click
Treat unexpected links in WhatsApp or SMS with caution: verify the sender and destination before opening them, especially when a message asks for passwords, codes or payment.
☀️
Solar Saves More Than You Think
A correctly designed solar system may reduce dependence on grid or generator power. Savings depend on load, design, tariffs and site conditions.
💻
Clean Your PC Every 6 Months
Dust buildup causes overheating and slow performance. Regular maintenance extends computer life by 3-5 years.
⚡
Check Wiring Every 5 Years
Arrange qualified inspection after warning signs, damage or load changes. Do not ignore hot outlets, tripping or burning smells.
