Practical Guides & Tips

Practical Guides & Safety Tips

Practical technology, electrical safety and solar guides for homes, schools and businesses.

💡 Quick Tech Tips

🔐
Always Verify Before You Click
Treat unexpected links in WhatsApp or SMS with caution: verify the sender and destination before opening them, especially when a message asks for passwords, codes or payment.
☀️
Solar Saves More Than You Think
A correctly designed solar system may reduce dependence on grid or generator power. Savings and payback depend on load, system design, tariffs, fuel costs and site conditions.
💻
Clean Your PC Every 6 Months
Dust buildup causes overheating and slow performance. Regular maintenance extends computer life by 3-5 years and prevents costly repairs.
📱
Strong Password = Strong Protection
Use at least 12 characters mixing letters, numbers and symbols. Never use the same password for multiple accounts. Consider a password manager.
⚡
Check Wiring Every 5 Years
Arrange a qualified inspection after warning signs, damage, alterations or changes in load, and periodically on qualified advice. Do not ignore hot outlets, repeated tripping, burning smells or damaged cables.
🎓
Digital Records Never Get Lost
Paper records can burn, flood or fade. Cloud-based school result systems should use appropriate backups, access controls and recovery processes; check how your school manages these safeguards.
