Our Story

Rehoteq Technologies was founded in Okitipupa, Ondo State, with a clear mission: bring technology solutions that actually work for Nigerian conditions to people who need them most — particularly in communities larger tech companies overlook.

Our first product was the Rehoteq RMS — a cloud-based school result management system built from the ground up for Nigerian secondary schools. Teachers enter scores from any phone, results calculate automatically, parents access from anywhere. Designed to work on 3G, handle Nigerian term/session structures and be affordable for schools in towns no other tech company marketed to.

From there we expanded into solar PV engineering, electrical installation, cybersecurity (RehoCheck), AI education tools (AI Lesson Studio & Academic AI), voice technology (VoiceCoach NG), device diagnostics (PhoneLab) and computer hardware services. Each product feeds the next: field engineering creates data for calculators, calculators drive Academy guides, guides create audience for Insight reports, reports attract professional partners for Labs.

Today Rehoteq serves homes, schools and businesses across South-West Nigeria and users worldwide online — still building from Okitipupa, still designed for Africa.
