Project overview

Work in progress, with a clear educational purpose.

The project is an ongoing modern Computer and ICT Laboratory located within the Ekiti State University compound. The photographs on this page show a construction-stage view of the work, including masonry walls, formwork, reinforcement grids and conduit- or pipe-like runs.

They are provided as a project update only. They are not a structural inspection, engineering certification, completion notice or commissioning statement.
