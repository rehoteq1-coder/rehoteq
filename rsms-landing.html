What RSMS Does

Every Tool Your
School Needs

Not a half-finished foreign software adapted for Nigeria. Built here, for here.

📊
Results & Report Cards
Teachers enter CA1, CA2 and exam scores. RSMS auto-calculates totals, assigns grades (A1–F9), generates single-page A4 result sheets with class teacher remarks, conduct, and interest ratings.
All Plans
📱
QR Code Attendance
Each student gets a unique QR card. Class teacher scans with phone camera — attendance marked instantly. Absent students' parents receive WhatsApp alerts automatically.
Standard+
💰
Fee Management
Bursar records cash and transfer payments. Parents pay online by card or bank transfer — payment reflects instantly on student and parent portals. Full cashbook and receipts.
All Plans
📚
CBT Examination Platform
Upload question banks per subject. Students take timed exams on phone or computer. Questions randomised per student. Results scored and displayed automatically the moment exam is submitted.
Standard+
👨‍👩‍👧
Parent Portal
Every parent gets a portal to see their child's results, attendance records, fee status, assignments, and school messages — real time, from any phone, no app download required.
All Plans
📋
HOD & VP Workflow
Teachers submit lesson plans, notes, and exam papers digitally. HOD reviews and approves or returns with comments. VP gives final approval. Full audit trail — principal sees everything.
Standard+
🏫
Class Streams & Arms
Configure JSS 1A, 1B, 1C, 1D — up to 6 arms per class. Each class teacher manages their specific arm. Scores, attendance, and remarks are arm-specific.
Standard+
📡
GPS Staff Clock-In
Staff clock in and out from their phones. GPS verification confirms they are on school premises. Late arrivals flagged automatically. Full monthly attendance reports for payroll.
