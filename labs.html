Live Products

In production, with real users.

Rehoteq RMS — School Result Management EdTechLive
Problem
Nigerian schools still run results on paper and fragile spreadsheets — errors, delays, and parents who can't see progress.
Solution
Cloud result management built for Nigerian terms and sessions: teachers enter scores from any phone, results, report cards and broadsheets generate automatically, designed to work on 3G.
Users
Secondary schools — administrators, teachers, parents and students.
Stage
Live and serving schools. Multi-tenant portals for staff and students.

RehoCheck — Link & Scam Checker CybersecurityLive on Google Play
Problem
Nigerian users are targeted by phishing links, fake news headlines and scam sites faster than awareness campaigns can keep up.
Solution
Free Android app: scan any link, website or news headline for threat signals in seconds, in plain language.
Users
Everyday Android users, schools and small businesses.
Stage
Published on Google Play; ongoing model and rule improvements.

CBT & Assessment Platform EducationLive
