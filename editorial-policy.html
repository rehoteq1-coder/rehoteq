Editorial Policy

This policy explains how we create and maintain practical guides for Nigerian readers. Last updated: 1 September 2026.

PurposeAuthorshipSourcesCorrectionsCommercial separationLimits

Purpose and scope

Rehoteq Editorial publishes practical explainers for Nigerian schools, households and small organisations. Our subjects include school administration, digital safety, solar planning and electrical safety. We aim to help readers understand a process, prepare useful questions and recognise when they need a qualified professional or an official source.

We do not present guides as live news, personalised professional advice, inspections, legal opinions, certifications or guarantees. A guide is not a substitute for a site visit, a manufacturer’s instructions, a school’s safeguarding process, a regulator’s current rule or a professional assessment.

Authorship and accountability

Articles are attributed to the Rehoteq Editorial Team. The editorial team is accountable for the published article: its clarity, sourcing, stated limits and corrections process. Attribution to the team does not imply that a named engineer, school, regulator or external professional personally reviewed a particular article unless the article explicitly says so. We do not make that claim by default.

We may use AI-assisted drafting and editing tools to help organise research, develop a first draft, improve readability or check consistency. Human editorial responsibility remains with Rehoteq: an editor is responsible for reviewing the final published wording, checking that sources are identified, removing unsupported claims and making corrections when needed. AI assistance is not treated as a source or an authority.

Sourcing and updates

When a guide refers to a rule, official process, product security setting or safety principle, we seek to link to primary sources where reasonably available: regulators, government bodies, examination bodies, manufacturers or official service providers. Each guide has a Sources section so readers can check the material directly.

Sources can change, be incomplete or apply differently to a particular case. We avoid invented quotations, statistics, testimonials and personal experience. We distinguish labelled hypothetical examples from real cases. We avoid claims of universal outcomes such as guaranteed savings, exam performance, security or compliance. If we cannot verify a point adequately, we remove it or describe the uncertainty.

We review guides when a material source changes, a credible correction is received or an editorial check identifies a substantive weakness. The displayed update date shows the most recent material editorial update, not a promise that every external source was checked on that day.

Corrections and feedback

We welcome specific correction requests. Please use the contact page and include the page URL, the passage you believe is inaccurate, the correction you propose, and a reliable supporting source if you have one. We will assess the report, correct a material error where warranted and update the page date when the change is material. We may not publish every suggestion, but we will not knowingly leave a material factual error unaddressed.

For urgent account compromise, electrical danger, medical symptoms, financial loss or a safeguarding concern, do not wait for an editorial response. Contact the relevant emergency, bank, provider, school or qualified professional through an official channel.

Commercial separation

Rehoteq Technologies offers technology and engineering services. Editorial guides may link to relevant Rehoteq pages where that link helps a reader navigate the site, but the guide should remain useful without buying a service. We do not sell placement inside a guide, accept payment for favourable coverage or allow a commercial relationship to decide what safety limitation or source is disclosed.

Where an article discusses an area in which Rehoteq provides services, that is not an endorsement that Rehoteq is right for every project. Readers should compare appropriate options, ask questions and seek site-specific professional advice where needed.

Safety, financial and educational limits

Electrical and solar content does not provide live-work instructions, cable sizes, protection settings or a design for a particular premises. Do not use it to alter wiring, connect a generator, bypass a protective device or work near live electrical equipment. Financial references and examples are educational only; they are not prices, forecasts, investment advice or purchasing recommendations. Education content does not guarantee examination outcomes and does not replace official instructions from examination bodies or a school’s own approved policies.

Reader responsibility: use our guides to ask better questions and identify authoritative help. Stop and seek competent assistance where a task involves danger, personal data, money, legal duties or a decision that could materially affect another person.
