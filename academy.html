Why learn here

Practitioners, not paraphrasers. Rehoteq content is written by a certified electrical & solar PV engineer and NSQ-trained assessor who installs systems and trains people for a living — and by an editorial team that cites primary sources and states limitations openly. Where a topic needs a licensed professional on site, we say so instead of pretending a web page can replace one. Following Nigeria's digital-education push, we prioritise practical digital skills, teacher competency and tools that work on real Nigerian connections and budgets.
