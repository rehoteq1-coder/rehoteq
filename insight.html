How Rehoteq Insight works — Methodology

  • Field-first: Every report starts from real deployments, site surveys or platform usage — not desk research alone. Solar Cost from real quotes, School Readiness from school visits, TVET from training assessments.
  • Transparent assumptions: We publish sample sizes, collection dates, geographic coverage and limitations. Where data is thin, we say so.
  • Open index: Nigeria Technology Index methodology and weights are public, so researchers can reproduce or critique.
  • No paywall on data: Key findings and index scores are free. Full datasets available to research partners under open terms.
  • Built in Okitipupa, for Nigeria: We deliberately start outside Lagos/Abuja to capture underserved realities — then expand nationally with partners.
