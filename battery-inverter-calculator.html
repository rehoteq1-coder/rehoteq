Energy and power are different questions

Backup energy (Wh) = Σ(watts × quantity × hours). Nominal bank Wh = backup Wh × (1 + reserve/100) ÷ (DoD × efficiency). Whole-bank Ah = nominal Wh ÷ nominal bank voltage. All listed loads are assumed to run together for the inverter power calculation, even if one row has zero backup hours.

Continuous inverter targets are total running W × 1.25 and that result ÷ aggregate PF in VA. Verify both the manufacturer’s continuous W and VA ratings. The 25% planning margin is not a motor-start allowance. Approximate running DC current = running W ÷ (bank V × efficiency); startup current and voltage sag can be higher.

Default worked example

Six 10 W lights for five hours use 300 Wh; two 60 W fans for six hours use 720 Wh; an 80 W TV for four hours uses 320 Wh; a 12 W router for eight hours uses 96 Wh. Total = 1,436 Wh. Running power = 272 W. With 20% reserve, 80% DoD and 90% efficiency, nominal energy is 2,393.33 Wh, or 49.86 Ah at 48 V. Continuous inverter targets are 340 W and 425 VA at PF 0.8. Choose equipment only after checking its surge and battery-current limits.

DoD is a planning choice, not a warranty claim

The 80% lithium and 50% lead-acid values are conservative example settings, not universal manufacturer limits. AGM, gel and tubular are kept separate for clarity but use the same planning DoD here. Lead-acid delivered capacity depends on discharge rate (Peukert effect), temperature, age and charge state. This simple model does not simulate those effects. Lithium packs also have temperature and BMS cutoffs.

Bank Ah is not “Ah per battery.” Series changes voltage without adding Ah; parallel changes Ah at the same voltage. Not all lithium packs permit series connection. This tool intentionally does not prescribe a wiring arrangement or count of batteries. Ask a professional to specify compatible modules, protection, ventilation, charging and emergency isolation.

What the estimate excludes

Enter actual electrical input watts, not an appliance’s marketing or cooling capacity. Hours should cover the outage you want to bridge; this is not necessarily the full day. Add inverter idle consumption to the energy allowance. The tool excludes solar yield, charging losses, days of autonomy, installation costs and detailed battery ageing. Reserve is a simple energy allowance, not a promise of extra runtime.

Source for battery/BMS compatibility and system limits: Victron Lithium Smart manual, system design. Its product-specific limits must not be applied to other brands. The energy equations above are dimensional calculations; the DoD, efficiency and reserve are our disclosed example assumptions.
