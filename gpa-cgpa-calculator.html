Formula and worked example

Quality points = grade point × credit units. GPA = semester quality points ÷ semester attempted units. CGPA = (previous + new quality points) ÷ (previous + new attempted units). Do not average semester GPAs unless their unit totals are identical.

The example has 3 units at 72 (15 points), 2 at 63 (8 points), and 1 at 38 (0 points): 23 ÷ 6 = 3.83 GPA. If previous totals are 120 quality points across 30 units, new CGPA is 143 ÷ 36 = 3.97. Full precision is retained until display, which rounds to two decimals.

Failed courses, repeats and missing marks

Failed-course units remain in this tool’s denominator. Enter only courses that your institution includes. Carry-over replacement, withdrawn courses, non-credit courses and result adjustments require your registry’s rules; this calculator does not infer them. A blank mark is an error, not an automatic failure. A valid zero mark is a failure. Fractional marks are not rounded before grading.

Do not enter JAMB/UTME scores here: this is a credit-weighted university or polytechnic planning tool, not an admissions aggregate. No class-of-degree promise is made. Primary authority: your institution’s approved academic handbook and registry-issued transcript; we intentionally do not label either preset as universally NUC-approved.
