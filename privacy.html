2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users. The data collected depends on your interaction with our website and apps:

2.1 Information You Actively Provide

  • Enquiries & Quote Requests: When submitting forms on our website or contacting us via WhatsApp/Email, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, location, and service requirements.
  • School Registration & RMS: Schools onboarding to RSMS provide institution name, administrative contacts, curriculum settings, and designated staff access credentials.
  • Interactive Tools & Calculators: Information inputted into our free engineering calculators (Solar PV sizing, electrical wiring, building estimation) is processed client-side in your browser and is not stored unless submitted via WhatsApp.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Log & Device Data: Browser type, operating system, device model, referring URLs, access timestamps, and pages viewed.
  • IP Address & Geolocation: Used for general regional traffic analysis, security diagnostics, and serving localized content.
  • Cookies & Tracking Identifiers: Standard browser cookies and anonymous identifiers used for site functionality, analytics, and advertising.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate business and educational purposes:

  • To provide, operate, maintain, and improve our website and software applications.
  • To respond directly to inquiries, customer support questions, and project quotes.
  • To prevent fraudulent activity, security vulnerabilities, and unauthorized use of our platforms.
  • To serve relevant, non-intrusive advertisements that help keep our educational tools free for Nigerian students, teachers, and homeowners.
  • To comply with legal obligations under applicable Nigerian and international laws.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like many websites, Rehoteq Technologies uses "cookies" (small text files saved on your device) and web beacons to store user preferences, optimize page load times, record session data, and measure website traffic.

Types of cookies used on our site:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for navigation, security, and authentication across our portals.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Help us understand aggregate user traffic patterns and optimize tool functionality.
  • Advertising & Third-Party Cookies: Set by advertising partners (including Google AdSense) to serve ads and evaluate ad performance.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings. However, doing so may affect the interactive functionality of certain features.

9. User Privacy Rights (NDPA, GDPR, CCPA)

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023), European GDPR, and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request copies of personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request deletion of your personal data where retention is not required by law.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object to the processing of your data for direct marketing or personalized advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at rehoteq@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children's Online Privacy Protection

Protecting the privacy of young children is especially important. Our public website, engineering calculators, and AI tools are designed for general audiences and secondary/tertiary students. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. In RSMS, student records are created and administered solely by authorized schools and guardians under institutional consent.
